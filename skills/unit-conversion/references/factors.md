# Conversion factors

Multiply by the factor to go from the left unit to the right one; divide to go back. Factors marked "exact" are definitions.

## Length

| From                | To              | Factor           |
| ------------------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| inch (in)           | centimetre (cm) | 2.54 (exact)     |
| foot (ft)           | metre (m)       | 0.3048 (exact)   |
| yard (yd)           | metre (m)       | 0.9144 (exact)   |
| mile (mi)           | kilometre (km)  | 1.609344 (exact) |
| nautical mile (nmi) | kilometre (km)  | 1.852 (exact)    |

## Temperature

Temperatures need an offset, so use these formulas:

- Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9 / 5 + 32
- Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5 / 9
- Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
